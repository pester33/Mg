# MG Menu

A hack menu for **PC and mobile**.

- **Noclip**: can't die (counts noclip deaths)
- **Speedhack**: 0.1x to 5x, with quick presets
- **Show Hitboxes**
- **No Death Effect**
- **Unlock All Icons** and colors in the garage
- **Info Overlay**: FPS, speed, noclip deaths
- **Instant Restart** after dying

**Open it:** the green **MG** button in the pause menu or on the main menu, or press **Tab** on PC (changeable in settings).

**Safe Mode:** as soon as you use noclip or speedhack in a level, completions and new bests for that visit **don't save**, so cheated runs can never count.
